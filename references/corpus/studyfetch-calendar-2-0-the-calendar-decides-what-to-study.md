## The move

Open on the pain, not the product: the first beat is the student deciding what to study tonight, and the calendar answers it - exam, topics, mastery - so the UI enters as the answer to a question the viewer already has.

## What it buys this film

StudyAI's hook is the same pain (page in, no idea where to start). Borrowing the problem-first order means the device beat proves something instead of introducing a screen.

## What I am NOT taking

the calendar metaphor itself; the film is about understanding a page, not scheduling.
