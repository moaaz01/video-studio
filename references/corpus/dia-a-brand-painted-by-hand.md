## The move

brand identity is shown as a living material: colour, wordmark and texture are painted/morphed in front of the viewer, then applied to the product in the same move

## What it buys this film

The design beat must prove the shop can look like the owner, not list 'customisation'. Borrow the paint-on-brand move: one accent swatch becomes the storefront's whole surface.

## What I am NOT taking

the surface style only — composition and timing are ours.
